Tianhe Wang

I am a C.V. Starr Fellow at the Princeton Neuroscience Institute. I completed my PhD in Psychology at UC Berkeley, advised by Rich Ivry. I study how the brain acquires new motor skills, combining behavioral experiments, neuropsychological testing, and computational modeling.
